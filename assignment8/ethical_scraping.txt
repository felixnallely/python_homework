#Refelct
It is important for websites to use robots.txt because it protects work from being stolen. 
Having a robots.txt helps reduce bots and crawlers on a website this keeps them from slowing down the performance of the page. 
A robots.txt acts as a doorman, making sure bots don't go into any restricted areas making sure information is kept safe.
